Race night

Mass starts on a loop course with one shared start and finish. Every category races about 45–60 minutes of laps.

  1. 5:00–6:15 pmCheck-in and number plate pickup at the Frozen Roads tents
  2. 6:00–6:15 pmCourse open for pre-riding. No pre-riding before 6:00.
  3. 6:25 pmPre-race meeting at the start line. Line up by category.
  4. 6:30 pmRace start. Categories go off at one-minute intervals.
  5. 7:45 pmSponsor swag giveaway once everyone's finished

The rules

Short list. Most of it is about keeping the Nordic trails in shape so we get invited back.

Fat tires: 3.8″ or wider

Skinnier tires race in a separate category. Run low pressure — high-pressure tires rut the groomed trail.

Clean bikes only

No dirt on the frame or tires — a dirty bike can't race. If spring brings mud, wash it before you arrive.

Helmet and lights required

Parts of the course have no overhead lighting. Bring a bright handlebar light, helmet light, or both.

Soldier Hollow waiver

Every racer needs a digitally signed Soldier Hollow Nordic Center waiver. You can sign it during registration.

Results

Results may not be posted on race night. Check back here the next day.

Be good out there

No prize purse. Kudos go to the friendliest, most supportive riders on course.
